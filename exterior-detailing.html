Shine Elite provides professional mobile exterior detailing across Canada, delivering top-tier car care to your doorstep. Our advanced techniques safely and effectively remove dirt, grease, road salt, and other contaminants while preserving your vehicle's paint and finish.

Our premium exterior detailing service enhances your car's appearance and protects it from environmental damage at an affordable price. We follow a meticulous process to decontaminate your paint and safely remove pollutants, water spots, residue, salt buildup, asphalt, and more.

Our Exterior Detailing Process

We follow a meticulous 4-step process to ensure your vehicle's exterior is thoroughly cleaned, protected, and restored to a showroom-quality shine.

1. Hand Wash

We thoroughly hand wash and dry the exterior of your vehicle to make sure that dirt and contaminants are properly rinsed away.

We decontaminate your paint using a clay bar to remove all loose dirt and microscopic contaminants embedded on the surface of the paint's clear coat.

Once clean, the paint is then carefully protected using wax to help enhance gloss while also leaving behind a layer of protection.

Premium Wax Protection

Our premium wax application enhances gloss and leaves behind a durable layer of protection against environmental contaminants.

2. Wheels & Tires Cleaning

The wheels are carefully hand cleaned and the tires are meticulously cleaned and dressed.

We ensure every wheel well is thoroughly cleaned and protected from brake dust and road grime.

We will even clean your engine bay if needed to ensure a complete exterior transformation.

Complete Wheel & Tire Care

Our meticulous wheel and tire cleaning removes brake dust and road grime, leaving them looking like new.
