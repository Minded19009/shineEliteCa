Additional Detailing Services

Home / Services / Additional Detailing Services

Ozone Odor Removal

Depending on the severity of the odour within your vehicle, our ozone treatment can remove smells from smoke, pets, gasoline and many other types of odours.

Iron Decontamination & Clay Bar Treatment

Tiny iron particles, brake dust, and road grime can bond to your car's paint over time causing rough texture and long-term damage. Our two step process includes Iron Remover to break down embedded contaminants and Clay Bar Treatment to pull out remaining impurities from the paint surface.

Pet Hair Removal

Pet hair can be stubborn and difficult to remove from your car's interior, getting trapped in carpets, seats, and upholstery. Our pet hair removal service uses specialized tools and techniques to thoroughly eliminate embedded fur, leaving your vehicle clean, fresh, and allergen-free. Enjoy a hair-free ride with our expert detailing service.

Headliner Cleaning

Your vehicle's headliner (roof lining) can collect stains, odors, smoke residue, and dust over time. Our headliner cleaning service gently removes dirt and grime without damaging the delicate fabric or adhesive. We use safe, effective products that restore freshness and improve the overall interior look and air quality of your car.

Deep Exterior Clean & Polish

Our comprehensive exterior cleaning includes Contactless Car Wash for safe & scratch-free cleaning, Two Bucket Hand Wash for thorough & gentle paint care, Iron Decontamination & Complete Clay Bar Treatment to remove embedded contaminants, and Sealant or Hand Wax for long-lasting protection & deep gloss.

Engine Bay Cleaning

Your engine deserves care too! Over time, dirt, grease, and debris accumulate under the hood, affecting performance and appearance. Our engine bay cleaning service uses safe, non-damaging techniques to degrease and refresh your engine compartment leaving it clean, organized, and showroom-ready. A clean engine not only looks better but can also help spot leaks and improve maintenance visibility.
