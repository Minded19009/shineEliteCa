Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions Answered — Shine Elite Detailing FAQs

1. Do you offer interior-only or exterior-only packages?

Yes, we offer both interior-only and exterior-only packages. Our interior detailing package focuses on deep steam cleaning of all surfaces, including seats, carpets, dashboard, and door panels. Our exterior detailing package includes a thorough hand wash, clay bar treatment, wax application, and wheel/tire cleaning. You can choose the service that best fits your needs.

2. What makes detailing different from a regular car wash?

A regular car wash focuses primarily on exterior surface cleaning, while detailing is a comprehensive service that includes deep cleaning of both interior and exterior, paint correction, ceramic coating application, stain removal, and attention to every nook and cranny. Detailing restores your vehicle to showroom-ready condition and provides long-lasting protection.

3. What's included in your premium detailing packages?

Our Platinum Package includes a full exterior wax shampoo wash, wheels and tires cleaning, complete interior vacuum and steam cleaning, leather reconditioning, salt stain removal, and interior/exterior window cleaning. It's a complete showroom-ready transformation for your vehicle.

4. Do you use eco-friendly cleaning products?

Absolutely! At Shine Elite, we prioritize the care of your vehicle, our team, and the environment. That's why we use eco-friendly products and advanced cleaning techniques to deliver a spotless shine while reducing our footprint. Our chemical-free steam cleaning removes dirt and grime without harsh chemicals or leaving wastewater behind.

5. Can you remove scratches and swirl marks from my car's paint?

Yes! Our paint correction services, including Stage 1, 2, or 3 polishing, can remove light scratches, swirl marks, and paint imperfections. We use professional-grade compounds and polishers to restore your paint's clarity, depth, and showroom shine. We also recommend ceramic coating after correction for long-term protection.
