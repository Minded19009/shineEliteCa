Ceramic Coating

Home / Services / Ceramic Coating

Ultimate Ceramic Coating Protection in Canada

Say goodbye to waxing! At Shine Elite, our ceramic coating service offers long-term protection for your vehicle's paint. This advanced coating minimizes maintenance, keeps your car's paint looking flawless, and provides a superior shield against dirt, UV rays, and contaminants — far exceeding traditional waxes or sealants. We use premium ceramic coatings to meet and exceed our clients' expectations, ensuring a long-lasting, high-gloss finish and maximum protection.

Our Ceramic Coating Process

Step 1

Exterior Hand Wash

We thoroughly hand wash and dry the exterior of your vehicle to make sure that dirt and contaminants are properly rinsed away.

Step 2

Stage 1, 2 or 3 Polishing

Depending on the needs of the car and what the client is looking for, we will perform stage 1, 2 or 3 polishing to correct the paint.

Step 3

Ceramic Coating Application

We will apply gtechniq ceramic coating to protect the vehicle for up to 5 years, delivering a high-gloss, hydrophobic finish.

Step 4

Paint Correction & Preparation

Our multi-stage polishing process removes imperfections, swirl marks, and light scratches to prepare your paint for ceramic coating.

Step 5

Curing & Protection

The ceramic coating is allowed to cure, forming a durable, hydrophobic layer that protects against UV rays, chemicals, and contaminants.

Step 6

Quality Assurance

We perform a final quality check to ensure your vehicle's paint is flawless, glossy, and protected with maximum durability.

Time Required (Approx.): 9 to 16 Hours
